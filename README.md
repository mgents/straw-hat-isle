# Straw Hat Isle

A little 3D pirate game. Pick one of the ten Straw Hats, change their clothes and
gear, then land on a training island and smash things with their own signature move.

**Play it: https://mgents.github.io/straw-hat-isle/**

Works in a browser on a computer or an iPad. On a touchscreen use the stick in the
bottom-left and the buttons in the bottom-right; on a keyboard use WASD, J to punch,
Shift to dash, Space to jump, K to kick and E for the special move.

Built with Unity 6. Source lives separately; this repo holds the published WebGL build.
